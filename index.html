How it's computed — χ² uses the standard 2×2 formula n(ad−bc)²⁄[(a+b)(c+d)(a+c)(b+d)], optionally with Yates' correction; its p-value comes from the exact df = 1 relation to the normal distribution. Fisher's exact p-value sums hypergeometric probabilities of every table at least as extreme as the observed one, given the fixed margins — no large-sample assumption needed. Odds ratio and relative risk use 95% Wald confidence intervals on the log scale. Rule of thumb: prefer Fisher's exact test whenever any expected cell count falls below 5.
